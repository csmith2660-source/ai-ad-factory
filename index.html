01The Problem
The Problem

More ads is not the answer.

01
Creative Similarity

Meta penalizes ads that look and feel the same. Same visual + different headline ≠ diversity.

02
Same angle. Same results.

You're probably over-indexed on specific audiences/angles in your ads

03
Wasted Spend

Scaling spend on repetitive creative just means paying more for diminishing returns.

The real unlock is creative diversity.

02The Framework
Creative Diversity

Five dimensions to drive diversity

Each dimension you vary creates a genuinely different ad. Cross-multiply them and one product branches into dozens of non-overlapping concepts.

01

Product

What you're selling.

The specific SKU, offer, or service.

02

Persona

Who you're talking to.

Their specific situation and psychology.

03

Angle

What you're saying to them.

The pain point/desire you're speaking to

04

Awareness

Where they are in the journey.

Unaware → Most Aware

05

Format

How the ad looks.

Testimonial, comparison, stat block, manifesto, UGC.
